# Look: Luxury real estate

**When to use:** high-end listings, interiors with natural light, agent brand films, anything that should feel calm,
expensive and editorial. Not for fast tips, hype, or busy talking-head lists.

**Fonts (shipped in `fonts/`):** Playfair Display 400 + 400 italic (headlines, captions), Inter 300 (small caps labels,
wide letter-spacing). Both SIL OFL, from the HyperFrames font cache.

**Colors:** ink `#0E0D0B`, cream `#F4EDE1`, gold `#C9A86A`. Grade: slightly desaturated, a touch more contrast.

**Captions:** sentence case, up to 4 words, Playfair 54 px cream; key words in gold italic. Lower band (from chin + 400 px),
no box: a soft dark gradient band sits behind them. Never above the head (that space belongs to titles).

**Titles / hook:** centred stack above the head between two thin gold hairlines: small spaced caps line, big serif
line, gold italic line. Lines fade up out of a blur.

**Cards / lists / lower thirds:** no filled boxes. Thin gold rules top and bottom, centred serif items, small gold
diamond bullets. Lower third = name in Playfair + role in spaced Inter caps between hairlines.

**Transitions:** dissolves and slow blur fades only. No whips, flashes or shakes.

**Motion:** slow (0.8-0.9 s, sine ease). Whole clip has a slow push-in (4 %). No punch zooms.

**Effect codes that fit:** HK01 only if restyled; PU02 (restyled, no box); FX01 text behind head; FX02 magazine cover;
TR21 iris reveal; PU26 name card. Avoid: CP20/CP22, TR20 whip, TR22 flash, FX20 vertigo, BR20/HK21 chat cutaways.

**Music mood:** soft piano, strings or ambient cinematic, slow (60-90 BPM), sits 20+ dB under the voice.
