# Look: Bold & energetic

**When to use:** tips, hot takes, myth-busting, "3 things" lists, market updates, anything fast and opinionated where
energy matters more than elegance. Not for luxury listings or calm brand films.

**Fonts (shipped in `fonts/`):** Inter Tight 900 (everything big), Inter Tight 700 (small labels). SIL OFL.

**Colors:** ink `#0A0A0A`, hot pink-red `#FF2E63`, volt yellow `#FFE600`, electric blue `#1E40FF`. Grade: +30 %
saturation, more contrast.

**Captions:** ALL CAPS, 1-2 words at a time, Inter Tight 900 at 96 px, white with a thick black outline. Key word on a
tilted volt box with a hard black drop shadow. Placed mid-body (chin + 170 px), never on the face.

**Titles / hook:** one beat at a time; each beat is a solid color block (blue, pink, volt) tilted a few degrees with a
hard offset shadow, slamming in from 160 % scale.

**Cards / lists / lower thirds:** stickers. Each item is its own tilted solid block, colors cycle pink/blue/volt, hard
black offset shadow, no rounded corners. Lower third = black tag with volt text.

**Transitions:** whip pans (TR20), camera flash (TR22), hard cuts on the beat.

**Motion:** fast (0.12-0.18 s, back.out overshoot). Punch zoom on the video (112 %) on every list hit. No slow push.

**Effect codes that fit:** HK20 slam headline, CP20/CP22 captions, PU20 count-up, PU21 number wheel, FX20 vertigo,
TR20 whip, TR22 flash, CT01 comment CTA. Avoid: FX02 magazine, slow dissolves, serif type.

**Music mood:** upbeat electronic, trap or pop (110-140 BPM) with clear hits for the pops; still 14-18 dB under the voice.
