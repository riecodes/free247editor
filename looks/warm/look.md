# Look: Warm lifestyle

**When to use:** neighborhood and community stories, family homes, "day in the life", personal stories, first-time
buyers, cozy interiors, outdoor lifestyle. Anything that should feel friendly and human rather than corporate.

**Fonts (shipped in `fonts/`):** Fraunces 700/900 (soft chunky serif), DM Sans 500 (body), Great Vibes 400 (handwritten
script accents, one or two words max). All SIL OFL.

**Colors:** cream `#FFF6EA`, paper `#FFF9F0`, terracotta `#C8643B`, brown `#3B2A20`, peach tape `#F6C9A8`.
Grade: warm (light sepia, slight hue shift toward orange).

**Captions:** sentence case, up to 3 words, DM Sans 500 brown on a cream rounded pill, low on the frame (chin + 420 px).
Key words in Fraunces 700 terracotta.

**Titles / hook:** centred stack above the head: cream label pill, big Fraunces 900 line in cream, a script word in
terracotta Great Vibes. Lines rise with a little rotation and a soft bounce.

**Cards / lists / lower thirds:** paper notes with a peach tape strip, slight -2 deg tilt, square-ish corners, soft
brown shadow, handwritten script title, terracotta hand-drawn checks. Lower third = paper label with a script name.

**Transitions:** soft slides and gentle zoom-dissolves; occasional paper tear. No hard flashes.

**Motion:** friendly (0.3-0.5 s, back.out 1.4-1.6 = small bounce), gentle 2 % push over the clip.

**Effect codes that fit:** PU23 marker checklist, FX03 sticker cut-out, FX23 freeze-frame scrapbook, PU02 (restyled
paper), CT20 follow card. Avoid: CP22 slam, HK20, TR22 flash, UI recreations, dark glass cards.

**Music mood:** acoustic guitar, ukulele, soft indie or warm lo-fi (85-110 BPM), happy, low under the voice.
