# Look: Clean & minimal

**When to use:** explainers, process walk-throughs, market data, professional services, modern homes and anything
that should feel clear and trustworthy rather than flashy. Good default for talking heads with busy backgrounds.

**Fonts (shipped in `fonts/`):** Plus Jakarta Sans 300, 500, 700. SIL OFL.

**Colors:** stone `#1F2421` text, sage `#7E9C7A` accent, mist `rgba(250,250,247,.86)` frosted surfaces.
Grade: slightly lifted and desaturated.

**Captions:** sentence case, up to 5 words, Jakarta 500 at 42 px, dark text on a small frosted chip, left aligned in
the upper third (x 90). Key words bold with a soft sage highlighter underline. No color on words.

**Titles / hook:** compact left-aligned block on a frosted panel: light intro line, bold line, light sage line.

**Cards / lists / lower thirds:** frosted white rounded cards (20 px radius), dark text, small sage circle checks,
placed below the chin on the left so the speaker keeps the right half. Lower third = frosted chip, name 700 + role 300.

**Transitions:** straight cuts and short cross-fades. No whips, flashes, tilts or shakes.

**Motion:** calm (0.3-0.55 s, power2.out), small 6-10 px rises. No zooms, no overshoot.

**Effect codes that fit:** PU01/PU02 (restyled frosted), PU22 line graph, PU03 proof card, BR21 browser card,
UI06 browser window, CT20 follow card. Avoid: CP22, HK20, FX20, TR22, sticker or paper looks.

**Music mood:** light lo-fi, soft house or acoustic minimal (90-110 BPM), very low under the voice.
