The app's starting style. Notes saved in my-rules.md beat anything here.

- Format: vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.
- Captions: word by word. Key words in big bold caps, small filler words in lowercase. Exact
  spoken words, Taglish as spoken. White with a soft shadow.
- Caption position: upper third, clear of the app buttons at the bottom.
- Hook: one short, bold line of hook text in the first 3 seconds. Captions start only after the
  hook card is gone, so the two never share the screen.
- Pace: tight. Cut dead air, false starts and retakes; keep a short breath at each join.
- Graphics: minimal. Captions, a few zoom-ins on key words, and the hook.
- Color: natural, light correction only.
- Sound: voice first. Light sound effects on graphics and cuts if they help. No music bed.
